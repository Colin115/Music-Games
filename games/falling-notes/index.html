🎵 Falling Notes

⏱️ Catch the ___ note!   |   ⭐ Score: 0

How to Play

  • Goal: Catch the correct falling note and avoid the others.
  • The target note is shown at the top — catch only that note.
  • Avoid wrong notes and rests.
  • You have 3 lives:
    • Lose a life if you catch the wrong note/rest.
    • Lose a life if you miss the correct note.

Controls

  • ⬅️ ➡️ Arrow keys
  • 🖱️ Move your mouse
  • 👆 Drag your finger (touch screens)

Tip: The notes fall faster as you score points! 🎶
