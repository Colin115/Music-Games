How to Play

  • Goal: Find and click the note you’re asked to find.
  • Look at the note name on the screen.
  • Click the matching note on the board.
  • Each correct answer gives you points and makes the game a little harder.

Tip: The notes may get smaller as you level up — stay sharp! 👀🎵
